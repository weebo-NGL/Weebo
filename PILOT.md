# TraceBrief support pilot

**$49 USD once for 30 days of setup support and evaluation on one repository.** For a team already running Playwright in GitHub Actions, the pilot answers a practical question. When a test fails, does a short PR comment help the engineer choose the next debugging step sooner?

## Try it on five failures for free

Your team may copy and run this release internally to evaluate up to five past failures. You can run TraceBrief yourself and share the resulting comments, or arrange private review of redacted Playwright JSON reports. For each report, you receive the evidence and one suggested next check. Compare it with what the investigating engineer actually did. No payment details are needed for this evaluation.

## What the $49 covers

- **Setup on one repository.** We help you add the JSON reporter alongside your existing reports, wire the Action into one workflow and verify the output together. A maintainer on your team approves the workflow changes.
- **One 30-day pilot.** The repository and dates are agreed before invoicing. The Action writes a job summary and can update a PR comment when permissions allow. Your team may copy the Action into that repository and continue running that copy there after the pilot.
- **One end-of-pilot review.** We look at the comments your engineers used, misleading suggestions and time spent reaching the first useful debugging step. You receive a short written recap.

Local analysis runs by default. If your team enables OpenAI suggestions, it supplies its own API key, pays its own API usage and controls whether excerpts may be sent. The $49 covers the service described above. A further service period requires a new agreement.
